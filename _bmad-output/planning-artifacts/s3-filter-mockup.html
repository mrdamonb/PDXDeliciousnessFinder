Trade-offs

✅Always discoverable — filters are the first thing you see below the header
✅Honors "never a modal" principle from UX spec — filter state visible at all times
✅Map/list toggle naturally lives in the header alongside the avatar
⚠️Map loses ~44px of vertical real estate — meaningful on a 13" laptop, less on a 27" monitor
⚠️Feels slightly busier when no filters are active (empty chips always visible)

Direction 6

Trusted Circle

Social-forward without being a social network. Friend attribution is more prominent — friend avatars on the map, "via [friend]" callouts on cards, a Friends-integrated map layer toggle. Emphasizes the "trusted circle" value prop.

👥 Personality

Warm, connected, trust-forward. The social layer is visible everywhere but never feels like a social network. Friend picks feel like recommendations whispered at a dinner party.

🎨 Visual Weight

Medium. Friend badges and "via" callouts add a social texture layer. Purple accents complement the earthy palette without dominating.

Spotify collaborative playlists + iMessage contact UI. Social but intimate. Connected but not public.
